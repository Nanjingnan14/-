## L9 Rural-urban Inequalities and Development（W9，课件 17 张）

### 概念与理论

| 概念／理论 | 课件定义（张号） | 一句话定义 | 老师解释的原句（录音位置） |
|---|---|---|---|
| global city | 「Saskia Sassen, The Global City: New York, London, Tokyo (1991)」；「Cities, rather than states, are now the key spatial unit in processes of globalization」（L9 第 2、4 张） | 全球化下取代国家成为关键空间单位的城市。 | "She defines global cities as having four key functions. These are the command posts she argues in the world economy."（W9 03:54） |
| new spatial order | 「A new spatial order (空間秩序) is emerging under globalization」（L9 第 4 张） | 全球化下正在形成的新空间秩序。 | "She argues that this is a new spatial order that is emerging under globalization."（W9 03:41） |
| global city 的四项功能 | 「1. Command posts in the world economy 2. Key locations for finances, insurance, real estate, accounting, advertising, engineering, architectural design, etc. 3. Sites for innovation; headquarters of firms 4. Markets for products and innovations」（L9 第 4 张） | 指挥、金融专业服务、创新总部、产品市场四处。 | "global cities are also sites of innovation. They are where all the headquarters of companies are based."（W9 04:52） |
| new geographies of centrality | 「New geographies of centrality at the global level bind together major international financial and business centers」（L9 第 5 张） | 以金融商务中心连成的新中心性地理。 | "So this gives birth to a new geography of centrality at the global level, finding together all the major international financial and business centers."（W9 05:32） |
| Ny-lon-kong | 课件只给短语：TIME Magazine (2008): Ny-lon-kong（L9 第 5 张） | 纽约、伦敦、香港三城连成一个节点。 | "NY is of course New York, LON is of course London, and Kong is Hong Kong."（W9 06:20） |
| polarization between rural and urban areas | 「Sharpening inequality between cities vs. surrounding rural areas」；「Concentration of development and opportunities in urban areas drain talents and resources away from rural areas」（L9 第 6 张） | 城市与周边农村差距拉大，人才资源外流。 | "we do see very clear, very clearly sharpening inequalities between cities and surrounding areas."（W9 07:12） |
| polarization in the city | 「High-income sectors: banking, investment, management, professionals, research and development, administration, etc.」；「Low-income group providing low-skilled services: rural migrants, immigrants」（L9 第 8 张） | 城内高收入部门与低技能移工两极并置。 | "You have high income sectors on the one end and the low income group providing low-skilled services, mainly performed by rural migrants and also by immigrants."（W9 13:07） |
| finance and services complex | 「The ascendence of specialized service-led economy, especially the new "finance and services complex" (Sassen 2000)」（L9 第 7 张） | 以金融与专业服务为核心的新产业综合体。 | "On the one end, we have the ascendance of a very specialized service-led economy, especially the new finance and services complex."（W9 11:02） |
| super-profit-making capacity | 「Super-profit-making capacity of leading industries based on the deregulation of markets (neoliberalism) and hypermobility of money」（L9 第 7 张） | 领先产业靠去管制与资金高速流动取得超额利润。 | "These industries have what Sasuke Sassan called super profit-making capacity, right? Generating a lot of revenue."（W9 11:07） |
| global care chains | 「Women as low-wage migrant workers, sending remittances back home which became an important source of hard currency for the migrants' home governments」（L9 第 9 张） | 女性移工跨国提供照护并汇回外汇的链条。 | "The rise of these global care chains, right? And it's a very strong gender dimension, right?"（W9 13:38） |
| feminization of survival | 「→ The "feminization" of survival」（Sassen 2002: 255-265）（L9 第 9 张） | 生存回路越来越建立在女性身上。 | "these kind of global care chains, right, built on the exploitation of a lot of women in developing countries and what Sassan calls the feminization, all right, of these survival circuits"（W9 15:12） |
| platform economy | 「Platform economy: Economic activity generated by online platforms (Kenney and Zysman 2016)」（L9 第 10 张） | 由线上平台产生的经济活动。 | "And that generates an entire new industry, right? Platform economy."（W9 16:10） |
| employment buffer | 「In China, platform economy acting as employment buffer for loss of jobs in construction and manufacturing sectors」（L9 第 10 张） | 平台经济吸收建筑与制造业流失的就业。 | "the platform economy is going to offer and employment buffer, all right, for all the loss of jobs in the construction sector, in the manufacturing sector"（W9 17:03） |
| infrastructural capitalism | 「a form of capitalism that is built on the production and expansion of intersecting physical and digital infrastructures, either spearheaded by or aided significantly by the Chinese state, which transforms labor processes and labor relations in the process」（Lin and Pun 2021: 651）（L9 第 13 张） | 靠物理与数字基础设施交汇而成立、改变劳动关系。 | "they coined the term infrastructural capitalism, referring to a form of capitalism that is built on the production and extension of intersecting physical and digital infrastructures"（W9 20:38） |
| physical／digital／human infrastructure | 「Physical infrastructure: Roads, Railways, Logistics networks, Cities」；「Digital infrastructure: E-commerce, Digital platforms, Data networks」；「Human infrastructure: Workers, Labour, Skills」；「Digital platforms do not operate in isolation.」（L9 第 14 张） | 物理、数字、人力三层基础设施互相依存。 | "it's made possible by three kinds of infrastructure."（W9 22:12） |
| hypercirculation of commodities | 「The hypercirculation of commodities in China is thus underpinned by the exploitation of a highly mobile migrant labor force excluded from fixed social reproduction in cities」（Lin and Pun 2021: 648）（L9 第 15 张） | 商品高速流通靠被排除在城市社会再生产外的移工。 | "this hyper circulation of commodities in China at this speed and at this volume is underpinned by the exploitation of a highly mobile migrant labor force who are often excluded from fixed social reproduction in cities"（W9 23:43） |
| precarious employment 的转移 | 「This transition moved migrant workers from one kind of precarious employment to another」；「Manufacturing jobs: short-term contracts, unstable employment security」；「Logistics work: independent contracting, possibly worse in labour protection」（L9 第 15 张） | 从制造业不稳定就业转到保障更差的物流工作。 | "But nowadays a lot of these migrants don't work in manufacturing jobs anymore. Nowadays there are a lot of them in logistics work, right?"（W9 24:15–24:26） |
| logistics chain 作为新的抗争场所 | 「The logistics chain emerging as a "new locus of migrant workers' struggles"」(Lin and Pun 2021: 651)（L9 第 15 张） | 物流链成为移工劳动政治的新场所。 | "the logistic chain is now emerging as a new locus of workers' struggles"（W9 24:50） |

### 人名与主张

| 人名 | 是谁 | 主张 | 出处 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Saskia Sassen | The Global City: New York, London, Tokyo (1991) 的作者，地理学者（逐字稿称 as a geographer） | 城市而非国家是全球化的关键空间单位；全球城市有四功能；金融与服务复合体的超额利润抬高商业空间价格；第三世界以女性移工建 survival circuits | L9 第 2、4、7、9 张；W9 00:00–03:41 |
| Kenney and Zysman | 2016 年给出 platform economy 定义的作者 | platform economy＝由线上平台产生的经济活动 | L9 第 10 张 |
| Lin and Pun | 2021 年提出 infrastructural capitalism 的作者 | 该资本主义建于物理与数字基础设施的交汇，过程改变 labor processes 与 labor relations；超流通靠被排除在固定社会再生产之外的移工 | L9 第 13–15 张 |
| Pun Ngai（逐字稿作 Pan Yi／Pan Ngai） | 曾在 PolyU 任教、现在 Lingnan University，长期研究劳工 | 与 Lin 合写 infrastructural capitalism，作为思考中国经济形态的工具 | W9 19:50–20:23（课件未列名） |
