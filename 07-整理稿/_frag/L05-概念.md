|---|---|---|---|

| 反发展／超越发展 anti-development / beyond development | 第 14 张：“Total rejection of development: views it to be a Eurocentric discourse and an imperialist project” | 把发展整体判为欧洲中心话语与帝国主义计划 | “it completely rejects development, it thinks that this whole idea of ... is itself a Eurocentric discourse and it is an imperialist project”（W5 第 329–332 行） |
| 后现代主义 postmodernism | 第 16 张：“Postmodernism: There is no objective reality outside of language, only different social constructions or local narratives” | 语言之外无客观现实，只有社会建构与本地叙事 | “post-modernism sets, actually there is no objective reality, because there is no reality outside of language, and language is something that is always specific”（W5 第 367–369 行） |
| 话语 discourse（論述／話語） | 第 20 张：“A group of statements which provide a language for talking about a particular topic at a particular historical moment” | 为谈论某主题提供语言的一组陈述 | “It's a group of statements which provide a language for talking about a specific topic at a particular time... | 权力—知识联结 power-knowledge nexus | 第 20 张：“Directly linked to power: power-knowledge nexus where “people and institutions have the power to define the terms of the debate or the way a problem is to be understood.” (Watson 2000: 70)” | 话语连着权力，机构可界定问题如何被理解 | “to Foucault, the field is never equal, because it's linked to power... | 福柯 Foucault（discourse、postmodernism） | 第 16 张：“Post-development theory is heavily influenced by postmodernism and the works of Michel Foucault”；第 20 张 discourse 定义整页 | 提出话语与权力—知识、影响后发展的后现代作者 | “you need to read him now for this class because it will be in your quiz”（W5 第 360 行；句子起于第 359 行）；同一段推荐 “Madness and Civilization”（W5 第 355–358 行） |
| 欧洲中心建构 Eurocentric construct | 第 21 张：“The way we speak about “development” and the “Third World” is largely a Eurocentric construct” | 谈发展与第三世界的说法大体出自欧洲中心 | “The way we talk about development, the way we talk about third world, all of that is actually the discourse of the West. | 知识形式 forms of knowledge | 第 23 张：“Three axes that define development as a domain of thought and action (p. 10): Forms of knowledge”；第 24 张：“The notions of “underdevelopment” and “Third World” were discursive products of the post-WW2 period (p. 31)” | 以专业知识界定何为欠发达的第一轴 | “Escobar argues that the notions of underdevelopment and third world were discursive products of World War II... before 1945, they just don't have these concepts... | 贫困的问题化 problematization of poverty | 第 24 张：“Problematization of poverty: The essential trait of Third World = poverty；Solution = economic growth and development” | 把贫困定为第三世界的本质，以增长为解决 | “Third world basically encodes poverty. | 权力体系 system of power | 第 27 张：“The system “sets the rules that must be followed for this or that problem, theory, or object to emerge and be named, analyzed, and eventually transformed into a policy or a plan” (p. 41)” | 规定问题如何被命名、分析并转成政策 | “Once you've identified all these problems, Western countries then come in and set the rules, saying that you need to follow our rules”（W5 第 546–549 行） |
| 发展的制度化 institutionalization of development | 第 28 张：“The “institutionalization” of development”；“Became “an ever-expanding development business”, “a lucrative industry for planners, experts, and civil servants” (p. 46)” | 知识经机构、会议与顾问服务流通成产业 | “development become institutionalized. | 主体性形式 forms of subjectivity | 第 29 张：“The discourse of development also creates forms of subjectivity (主观性)” | 人据此把自己看成发达或欠发达 | “all of this discourse of development creates forms of subjectivity... | 反政治机器 anti-politics machine | 第 40 张：“Putting political realities out of sight, at the same time, performing its own political operation of strengthening the state's presence in the local region” | 遮蔽政治现实，同时强化国家在地方的在场 | “The answer, according to Ferguson, is because they're deliberately putting the political realities out of sight. | LDC 建构（建构为「Less Developed Country」） | 第 33 张：“Lesotho was constructed by development agencies as a “Less Developed Country” (LDC)”；第 36 张：“largely standardized packages” | 贴上 LDC 标签，对象才匹配现成方案 | “They need to make it out to be an LDC, the construction of Lesotho as a generic, less developed country. Make Lesotho out to be an enormously promising candidate for the only sort of intervention”（W5 第 837–839 行） |
| 对发展的替代 alternatives to development | 第 42 张：““Interested not in development alternatives but in alternatives to development, that is, the rejection of the entire paradigm altogether” (Escobar p. 215)” | 不要发展的替代方案，要替代发展本身 | “What they want to do is not even development alternatives. | 多元宇宙 pluriverse | 第 47 张：““Pluriverse”: multiple co-existing worlds” | 多个世界共存，拒绝单一现代世界 | “So plural reverse says that we can have multiple co-existing worlds”（W5 第 1101 行；转写把 pluriverse 听成 plural reverse）；同段把 modernization approach 说成 “a one world world”（W5 第 1091–1092 行） |
| 新知识政治 new politics of knowledge | 第 48 张：“New knowledge politics: “Moving away from developmental sciences… from conventional Western modes of knowing in general in order to make room for other types of knowledge and experience” (p. 216)” | 离开西方认知模式，为其他知识留位 | “basically what this requires is a new politics of knowledge... | 从下往上看 from below | 第 48 张：“It looks at and experiences the world from below rather than from above. | 草根团体与反对力量大联盟 grassroots groups / grand coalition | 第 44 张：“Escobar envisions a grand coalition of opposition forces made up of “peasants, urban marginals, deprofessionalized intellectuals””；第 45 张四角标 Local、Pluralistic、Distrust organized politics、Distrust the Development establishment | 农民、城市边缘者与去职业化知识分子的联盟 | “He envisioned Escobar, a grand coalition of opposition forces made up of primarily peasants... urban marginals... deep professionals, intellectuals”（W5 第 990–995 行） |
| 国家收缩 shrinking the state | 第 6 张：“SAP “reversed” the development project as it prescribed “shrinking the state”” | SAP 以缩小国家反转战后发展计划 | “neoliberalism reversed all of that, it's a complete U-turn of the development project, because neoliberal approach is about shrinking the state”（W5 第 239–241 行） |
| 对后发展的四条批评 critiques of post-development | 第 50 张：“Complete rejection of modernity and development”；“Ignores many positive aspects development brought about”；“Uncritical stance towards local communities and cultural tradition”；“Rejecting universalism and celebrating cultural difference → danger of concealing or accepting the oppression legitimized by tradition/culture” | 拒绝过彻底、忽略发展成果、浪漫化传统 | “post-development theories clearly demonize this kind of industrial modernity.
