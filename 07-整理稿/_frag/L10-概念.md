## L10 Food, Agriculture and Development（W10，课件 27 张）

### 概念与理论

| 概念／理论 | 课件定义（张号） | 一句话定义 | 老师解释的原句（录音位置） |
|---|---|---|---|
| Food security | 「Food security exists when all people, at all times, have physical and economic access to sufficient, safe and nutritious food that meets their dietary needs and food preferences for an active and healthy life」(World Food Summit, 1996)（L10 第 6 张） | 所有人任何时候都能取得足够、安全、有营养的食物 | 「So a society or a community or a city is considered food secure when all people living at all times have physical and economic access to sufficient」（W10 07:05）；非食物条件：「But you don't have clean water … Then you still cannot turn that raw food into safe food」（W10 08:21） |
| Food self-sufficiency | 「Food self-sufficiency means 'the extent to which a country can satisfy its food needs from its own domestic production'」(FAO 1999; Clapp 2017)（L10 第 8 张） | 一国用本国生产满足自身食物需要的程度 | 「The extent to which a country can satisfy its food needs from its own domestic production.」（W10 13:45）；判据一例：「China pledged that they will from now on maintain a food self-sufficiency rate of 96%」（W10 13:28） |
| 100% food self-sufficiency line | 「100% food self-sufficiency line: production = consumption」（L10 第 8 张） | 生产量等于消费量的对角线，线上即完全自给 | 「On this line, that society produces just exactly as the amount that it consumes.」（W10 10:34）；「Production equals consumption.」（W10 11:00） |
| High food security but low self-sufficiency（两概念分叉） | 「A country can have high food security but low self-sufficiency」；「Singapore: Highest in food security index in 2019」；「But 90% of its food is imported from other countries (CAN 2019)」（L10 第 10 张） | 两者可背离：新加坡食安最高而九成靠进口 | 「because a community can have high food security but no self-sufficiency」（W10 14:23）；「in 2019, it has the highest food security in the world, but actually, 90% of its food is imported」（W10 14:43） |
| Food dependence | 课件只给短语：「Trade as a share of domestic food supply」，图例分 net importer 与 net exporter（L10 第 9 张） | 贸易占国内食物供应比重，分净进口与净出口 | 「So this is how it looks like in terms of food dependence」（W10 13:53） |
| Food loss and waste | 「One-third of all food produced for human consumption in the world (around 1.3 billion tons) is lost or wasted every year (FAO, 2011)」；45% of fruit and vegetables、35% fish and seafood、30% cereals、20% dairy、20% meat（L10 第 4 张） | 每年供人食用的食物有三分之一被损失或浪费 | 「one third of all the food we produce for human consumption every year is wasted」（W10 04:12） |
| Undernutrition／food insecurity（地图判据） | 课件只给短语：图层 UNDERNUTRITION 与 FOOD INSECURITY，图例为 Percent of the total population（0%-10% 至 >80%-100%）（L10 第 7 张） | 按人口比例分级的营养不良与粮食不安全地图 | 「the darker the color, the more people in that country is food insecure」（W10 09:13） |
| Traditional food system | 「Local production by local producers for local markets」；「Small scale, locally based production chain」；「High proportion of population involved in agriculture」；「Food are distributed within local boundaries」；「Consumption depends on harvests and seasons」（L10 第 14 张） | 本地生产者供本地市场的小规模食物体系 | 「You have local production by local producers for local markets. It's very small-scale.」（W10 19:21） |
| Green Revolution | 「1950s-1960s: 'Revolution' to remake Third World agriculture through a 'package' of plant-breeding agricultural technologies」；含 high-yielding varieties of hybrid seeds、chemical fungicides and pesticides、intensive irrigation and fertilization（L10 第 15 张） | 1950–60 年代以种子农药灌溉套餐重塑第三世界农业 | 「So what happened in the 50s and 60s that accounted for that sudden surge in world food production? All of that has to go back to the Green Revolution.」（W10 20:56） |
| Chemical treadmill | 课件只给短语：「Farmers incorporated into a 'chemical treadmill'」（L10 第 15 张） | 越用化学投入才维持产量、地力越薄的循环 | 「Why is it a treadmill? Because the more chemicals you use and the more you get from the land, the land become thinner」（W10 23:27） |
| Corporate food regime | 「Shift of control over food and agriculture away from nation-states and governments towards global capital」；「Characterizes the neoliberal world order」(McMichael 2009)；「Concentration of market power in a few giant corporations」（L10 第 16 张） | 食物与农业控制权由国家转向全球资本的体制 | 「So we see gradual shift of control over food and agriculture, away from nation states, away from governments」（W10 24:50）；「Global capital starting to take over」（W10 25:03） |
| The supermarket takeover（三波） | 「The average share of supermarkets in food retailing in the Global South rose from 10-20% (1990) to 50-60% (early 2000s)」；第二波 5-10%（1990）→30-50%（early 2000s）；第三波 10-20% by 2003（L10 第 18 张） | 超市在食物零售份额分三波上升的接管 | 「And we see supermarkets gradually take over.」（W10 26:48） |
| Second Green Revolution | 「Expansion of green revolution technology from basic foods to consumer foods and agro-industrial inputs」；「Specialized, high-input agriculture servicing high-value markets」（L10 第 19 张） | 绿色革命技术从基本粮食扩及消费食品与农工投入 | 「And then came what we called the second green revolution.」（W10 27:44） |
| High-value foods | 「High-value foods: off-season, exotic fresh fruits and vegetables as one of the most profitable markets for agribusinesses」（L10 第 19 张） | 反季与异域生鲜果蔬，农企业最赚钱市场之一 | 「So all of that high-value markets and also in food processing and agrochemical firms, we see more and more of these exotic fresh fruits and exotic fresh vegetables」（W10 28:13） |
| New division of world agricultural labour | 「Rise of land-based agricultural investments」；「Third World farmers face new conditions of work」（L10 第 20 张） | 以土地投资与出口标准重排南北农业劳动分工 | 「but I want to talk about the new division of world agricultural labor. So we're seeing increasing, again, North-South dynamic」（W10 29:35） |
| Land grabbing／global land rush | 「The acquisition or long-term lease of national land by foreign governments or companies for food and fuel production」；「Products usually exported to investing countries」；「Dubbed 'land grabbing' or the 'global land rush'」（L10 第 20 张） | 外国政府或公司收购、长租他国土地生产粮与燃料 | 「And so this has been called land grabbing or global land rush because in many cases the place where people lease the land for food exports are poor developing countries.」（W10 30:16） |
| Flexible contract labor | 课件只给短语：「farmers must work in the agribusiness sector as planters, pickers, packers → flexible contract labor」（L10 第 23 张） | 小农失去土地后进入农企业的可弃合同工 | 「as planters, pickers and packers and they are very flexible contract labor that are highly exploitable」（W10 33:08） |
| Drivers of change（Friedmann 2004） | 「Climate change: Unsustainable dependency on fossil fuel energy」；「Food safety」；「Biological diversity」；「Rise of social movements: Climate change, small farmers, land reform」（L10 第 24 张） | 气候、食安、生物多样性与社会运动四项变革动因 | 「Climate change, realizing that the way we're consuming is not sustainable.」（W10 34:56） |

### 人名与主张

| 人名 | 是谁 | 主张 | 出处 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Deborah Barndt（1997） | 追踪番茄从墨西哥到美国超市的研究者（逐字稿转写作 Deborah Grant） | 高峰季一农场 2,000 pickers 与 700 packers、数百名年轻女性跨站点流动；每日高剂量 pesticides；结论为 Northern consumers 的 food security 依赖 Mexican／southern producers 的 food insecurity | L10 第 23 张；W10 33:28–34:42 |
| Harriet Friedmann（2004） | 四项变革动因的作者 | climate change、food safety、biological diversity、rise of social movements 为食物体系变革的 driver | L10 第 24 张 |
| Philip McMichael（2009） | 企业食物体制论者 | 食物与农业控制权由 nation-states and governments 转向 global capital，此特征标志 neoliberal world order；市场权力集中于少数巨型公司 | L10 第 16 张 |
| Jennifer Clapp（2017） | food self-sufficiency 定义出处之一（与 FAO 1999 并列） | 主张 to be verified（课件只标引文，未展开） | L10 第 8 张 |
| Lester Brown | World Watch 的报告作者 | 1994／1996 追问「who is going to feed China」，警告中国人口增长与城市化工业化将占用世界资源；其后中国承诺维持 96% 自给率 | W10 12:59–13:37（课件未列） |
