## L3 Development and the Making of the Third World（W3，课件 60 张）

### 概念与理论

| 概念／理论 | 课件定义（张号） | 一句话定义 | 老师解释的原句（录音位置） |
|---|---|---|---|
| modernization approach | 「EACH COUNTRY IS RESPONSIBLE FOR ITS OWN DEVELOPMENT. RICH COUNTRIES DID THE RIGHT THINGS. POOR COUNTRIES SHOULD EMULATE RICH COUNTRIES.」（L3 第 3 张） | 各国自负发展之责，穷国照抄富国做法。 | "We've contrasted the modernization approach and the dependency approach."（W3 一段 19:55） |
| dependency approach | 「THE UNDERDEVELOPMENT OF POOR COUNTRIES IS THE RESULT OF INTERACTION BETWEEN GLOBAL POLITICS AND ECONOMY. POOR COUNTRIES ARE LOCKED IN A STATE OF DEPENDENT DEVELOPMENT.」（L3 第 3 张） | 穷国欠发达源于全球政经互动，且被锁在依附发展。 | "the level field is not fair. The playing field is not level, right?"（W3 一段 05:05） |
| Rostow's five stages of growth | 「Posited that every country in the world can be placed in one of five stages of economic growth」；终点为「a modern society characterized by "high mass consumption", representative of the US in the 1960s」（L3 第 4 张） | 各国可归入五个阶段，走完全程即达高额消费社会。 | "Talking about how development is this movement from one stage to another to grow from an agricultural society with traditional values and cultures to the age of high mass consumption"（W3 一段 04:01） |
| development of underdevelopment | 「Published in 1967」；「Question: Is it a coincidence that the rising prosperity and affluence in the West occurred during the same centuries as the conquest of the New World and the colonization of Asia and Africa?」（L3 第 5 张） | 西方繁荣与殖民征服同期，并非巧合。 | "And so all these three aspects, Andre Gouda Frank argues, right? Results in the underdevelopment of third world countries."（W3 一段 07:23） |
| metropolis-satellite model | 「Originated in the colonial era, when colonial powers implanted new cities in Third World countries to facilitate the transfer of economic surplus to the West」（L3 第 6 张） | 剩余沿城市层级由村庄抽往西方。 | "And there was the metropolis satellite model that he proposed to conceptualize how the surplus, right, is sucked out from villages to local capitals, to regional capitals, to national capitals, and finally enriching the Western powers."（W3 一段 07:34） |
| world-system theory | 「While dependency theory focuses on nation-states, Wallerstein takes the whole world system as the unit of analysis」（L3 第 7 张） | 以整个世界体系而非国家为分析单位。 | "Anyway, we also talked about Wallerstein's world system theory, right, and the special theoretical innovation of the semi-periphery, okay?"（W3 一段 08:22） |
| semi-periphery | 课件只给短语：Core／Semi-periphery／Periphery 三分（L3 第 7 张） | 居间一层，既被核心剥削也剥削边缘。 | "The semi-periphery helps to take political pressure off the core by doing two things."（W3 一段 08:54） |
| decolonization | 「Colonialism no longer tenable after WW2」；「Series of countries gaining independence after WW2, e.g. India from Britain in 1947 and Indonesia from the Netherlands in 1949」（L3 第 11 张） | 二战后殖民不再可行，多国相继独立。 | "By the end of World War II, colonialism is no longer sustainable."（W3 一段 11:01） |
| economic nationalism | 「Economic nationalism」；「Tried to reverse the colonial division of labour」；「Encouraged and protected domestic industries」；「Reduced dependence on primary exports」（L3 第 12 张） | 以国家力量扭转殖民分工、保护本国工业。 | "So, some scholars characterize this as the rise of economic nationalism."（W3 一段 14:25） |
| Bandung conference | 「1955: Bandung conference: First meeting of "non-aligned" Asian and African states in Indonesia」（L3 第 13 张） | 1955 年亚非不结盟国家首次集会。 | "In 1955, the Bandung Conference is seen as one of the most symbolic political events where all of these countries met together."（W3 一段 15:55） |
| Non-Aligned Movement（NAM） | 「1961: Formation of the Non-Aligned Movement (NAM)」；「Advocate economic self-reliance and non-interference in international affairs (based on "Five Principles of Peaceful Co-existence")」（L3 第 13 张） | 1961 年成立，主张自力更生与不干涉。 | "So anyway, so the non-aligned movement, we can call it NEA, was officially formed in 1961"（W3 一段 17:28） |
| UNCTAD | 「1964: Third World pressure further led to the establishment of the UN Conference on Trade and Development (UNCTAD), a platform for promoting Third World interests in world trade」（L3 第 14 张） | 1964 年设立，推第三世界贸易利益的平台。 | "And so OMTED was formed the United Nations Conference on Trade and Development."（W3 一段 18:48） |
| G-77 | 「Statement made by a Group of 77 non-aligned nations (G-77), demanding: Stabilized and improved commodity prices；Opening of First World markets to Third World manufactures；Expanding financial flows from First World」（L3 第 14 张） | 七十七国集团，要求稳定商品价格与开放市场。 | "non-aligned nations formed a group of 77 called GE77"（W3 一段 19:05） |
| free-market model | 「Based on modernization school」；「Capitalist」；「Private enterprise」；「Open to Western investment and imports under free trade model」；「Expansion of trade and investment as engine of growth」；「Direct state intervention only temporary」（L3 第 16 张） | 靠私人企业与自由贸易增长，国家只暂干预。 | "On the one hand, you have a free market model. This is of course the one championed by the United States, right?"（W3 一段 20:08） |
| state-led model | 「Draws on dependency theory」；「Soviet-inspired」；「Central planning」；「National self-sufficiency」；「Pessimistic on export growth, international competition, foreign investment」（L3 第 16 张） | 苏联式中央计划与自给自足，看淡出口。 | "you have the Soviet model, right? The Soviet model advocating a more state-led approach. Central planning."（W3 一段 20:49） |
| national industrialization | 「Both treat "national industrialization" as the vehicle of development」；「Assumed that development involved the displacement of agrarian civilization by an urban-industrial society」（L3 第 17 张） | 两模型共认国家工业化是发展载体。 | "There's a strong belief that if you want to develop, the first thing you need to do is you need to get rid of your agrarian civilization. Replace it with an urban industrial society."（W3 一段 22:29） |
| import-substitution industrialization（ISI） | 「"One of the 20th century's boldest and most widespread economic experiments"」；「Chile as the intellectual center of structuralism」（L3 第 23 张） | 以国产替代进口品的二十世纪大规模实验。 | "The logic behind ISI is very simple. It is still being used today."（W3 一段 30:54） |
| tariffs／quotas／content regulations | 「Strategy: Restrict foreign imports by measures such as (1) tariffs (2) quotas (3) content regulations」（L3 第 24 张） | 关税、配额、内容规制三件，抬高进口价。 | "All three policy instruments have the same effect... it makes foreign things more expensive."（W3 一段 33:37–33:43） |
| infant industries 与 licensing schemes | 「Governments also supported these infant industries through: Licensing schemes: limit the number of local firms allowed to produce a given product, in effect giving them a legal monopoly；Provision of subsidies and cheap credit」（L3 第 24 张） | 限厂商数目给法定垄断，另给补贴与低息信贷。 | "You're basically given a monopoly."（W3 一段 36:35） |
| achievements of ISI | 「In the 1930s, when the world economy was stagnating, Turkey's annual economic growth rate reached 7%」；「During India's first Five-Year Plan (1951-56), national income grew by 18%」（L3 第 26 张） | 1930 年代土耳其年增 7%、印度首五计划国民收入增 18%。 | "In the 1930s, when the world economy was not doing so well, right? Stagnating, Turkey saw an annual growth rate of 7%."（W3 一段 38:35） |
| ISI 的 inefficiency 与 poor export performance | 「Lack of competition →firms became inefficient, productivity was low」；「Being protected from competition, the quality of goods produced was simply too low to find market overseas」（L3 第 29 张） | 缺竞争致低效，产品出不了口。 | "There's no competition, right? So firms that come really inefficient and productivity was very low."（W3 一段 44:49） |
| ISI 的 corruption and inequality | 「Conducive to power abuse: Government officials granting license to reward supporters, demanding kickbacks in return」；「Inability of state to discipline capital」（L3 第 30 张） | 许可证换回扣，国家约束不住资本。 | "In South America, government officials ended up giving these licenses to their supporters."（W3 一段 47:41） |
| farewell to ISI | 「By the mid-1960s, there was growing disenchantment with ISI」；「Emergence and popularization of neoclassical economic theory」（L3 第 31 张） | 1960 年代中期不满增长，转向新古典与 EOI。 | "After several decades of experimenting with this, they realized that it's not working. It's not working."（W3 一段 49:35–49:43） |
| export-oriented industrialization（EOI） | 「Rather than building an industrial base to satisfy local demand, EOI focuses on export industries in manufactured goods →larger market」（L3 第 42 张） | 以制成品出口产业为主，面向更大市场。 | "Because they adopted a different approach to development, which we called export-oriented industrialization."（W3 二段 03:39） |
| newly industrializing economies（NIEs）／four Asian dragons | 「Newly industrializing economies (NIEs) as representative examples」；「"Four Asian dragons": Hong Kong, South Korea, Taiwan, Singapore」（L3 第 36 张） | 港、韩、台、新四地，出口导向的代表。 | "We call this group of economies the newly industrializing economies."（W3 二段 03:46） |
| developmental state | 「contrary to neoclassical theory which advocates a non-interventionist state, Asian NIEs all had governments that played an active role in guiding economic development (to a lesser extent in HK)」（L3 第 41 张） | 政府积极引导经济发展的国家形态。 | "But South Korea is a perfect example of a strong, what we call, developmental state."（W3 二段 09:37） |
| chooses winners／限期保护 | 「Rather than providing protection to all local industries, the government "chooses winners"」；「A time limit is placed on protection」（L3 第 42 张） | 只扶少数产业，保护限期，劣者淘汰。 | "Rather than protecting everyone, the government comes in and they choose winners."（W3 二段 13:00） |
| strong／hard state | 「Requires a "strong" or "hard" state that must be willing and able to impose discipline on private entrepreneurs」（L3 第 43 张） | 能对私人企业家施加纪律的强国家。 | "a strong and hard state that is willing and able to discipline private capital."（W3 二段 15:42） |
| new international division of labour（NIDL） | 「Decentralization of industrial production from the First World to the Third World」（L3 第 50 张） | 工业生产由第一世界分散到第三世界。 | "The actual production doesn't happen in the first world anymore."（W3 二段 30:00） |
| outsourcing／offshoring | 「First World companies closing existing factories at home and contracting production to "third party manufacturers", usually in developing countries」（L3 第 50 张） | 关掉母国工厂，把生产包给第三世界厂商。 | "They close factories at home, and they send production overseas. We call this outsourcing or offshoring."（W3 二段 26:10） |
| bifurcation of the global labour force | 「First World: Skilled labour: management, engineering, design, marketing」；「Third World: Deskilled labour: cutting, sewing, machine tending, assembly」（L3 第 50 张） | 全球劳动力分成技能与去技能两半。 | "And so we begin to see this, what we call a bifurcation, separation of the global labor force."（W3 二段 29:29） |
| global production systems／commodity chains | 「Goods are no longer produced within one country, and then traded」；「Global assembly line of multiple sites (commodity chains)」（L3 第 48 张） | 商品在多地点组装，不再由一国完成。 | "Many parts are going from one place to another. You have global assembly lines and multiple sites of production"（W3 二段 24:41） |
| export processing zone（EPZ）／free trade zone（FTZ） | 「Proliferation of export-processing zones or free trade zones」；「Enclaves created by governments to host foreign firms」；「Minimal customs controls, often free from labor regulations and local taxes」（L3 第 54 张） | 政府设的飞地，接外资、免劳工法规。 | "These are enclaves created by governments to host foreign firms."（W3 二段 34:22） |
