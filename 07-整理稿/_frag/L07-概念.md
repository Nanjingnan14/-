## L7 Gender and Development（W7，课件 19 张）

### 概念与理论

| 概念／理论 | 课件定义（张号） | 一句话定义 | 老师解释的原句（录音位置） |
|---|---|---|---|
| 性别分工（gender division of labour） | 课件只给短语：the gendered division of labour（L7 第 5 张） | 家庭内按性别分配劳务 | "thinking about the gender division of labor in your family"（W7 06:49） |
| 性别差距四维度 | "Economic Participation and Opportunity／Educational Attainment／Health and Survival／Political Empowerment"（L7 第 10 张） | 衡量性别平等的经济、教育、健康、政治四项 | "they basically focus on four main dimensions of gender equality"（W7 10:09–10:13） |
| practical gender needs | "Addressing practical gender needs, e.g. food aid, health, nutrition"（L7 第 11 张） | 食物援助、健康、营养一类日常需要 | "there were awareness on gender issues but a lot of the welfare measures targeting women mainly focus on their domestic role"（W7 13:11–13:20） |
| Women in Development（WID） | ""women's inequality of access to and participation in the definition of economic structures and policies and the productive process itself""（L7 第 12 张） | 妇女在定义经济结构与生产过程中的参与不平等 | "women has been contributing a lot to development it's just that their role is never formally recognized"（W7 14:35–14:45） |
| integrationist approach | "Women often perform unpaid household/farm labor → incorporate them more into income-earning activities"（L7 第 12 张） | 把从事无偿家务农活的妇女纳入收入性活动 | "So it approach the whole issue from an integrationist sort of method"（W7 16:01–16:04） |
| CEDAW | "Convention on the Elimination of All Forms of Discrimination against Women (CEDAW), adopted by UN assembly in 1979"（L7 第 12 张） | 1979 年联合国大会通过的消除对妇女歧视公约 | "there's a convention adopted by the UN Assembly in 1979 to remove these different forms of discrimination"（W7 16:41–16:47） |
| Gender and Development（GAD） | "Not enough to address who is doing what and whether it is valued → ask HOW activities come to be valued, by whom, and in whose interests"（L7 第 13 张） | 追问活动如何被赋值、由谁、为谁的利益 | "To ask how activities come to be valued by whom and in whose interest."（W7 18:57–18:59） |
| 社会再生产（social reproduction） | 课件只给短语：the invisible work of social reproduction（L7 第 13 张） | 育儿、家务、烹饪、教小孩等不可见劳动 | "All of these invisible work of social reproduction"（W7 19:36） |
| UN System of National Accounts | "UN System of National Accounts records only additions to GNP, but not "costs": the invisible work of social reproduction, costs to the environment, etc. No debit side."（L7 第 13 张） | 只记 GNP 增项、不记成本的国民核算 | "There's no cost in the UN system of accounts. There's no debit side"（W7 19:54–19:59） |
| agenda-setting approach | ""Agenda-setting" approach: Directly challenge conventional development thinking with a feminist perspective (Jahan 1995)"（L7 第 13 张） | 以女性主义视角直接挑战常规发展思维 | "We need to challenge all these conventional development thinking with a feminist perspective"（W7 20:50–20:54） |
| 经济效率的重新定义 | "Redefine economic efficiency to include and value women's unpaid and paid work"（L7 第 14 张） | 把妇女有酬与无酬劳动计入效率 | "To redefine economic efficiency, to include, to value, right? Women's unpaid and paid work."（W7 21:06–21:13） |
| 赋权（empowerment） | 课件只给短语：Changing gender relations through "empowerment"（L7 第 14 张） | 通过赋权改变性别关系 | "To empower women through transnational feminist networks"（W7 21:53–21:56） |
| 条件现金转移（conditional cash transfer） | "To "empower" women by giving them cash, in exchange for "co-responsibility""（L7 第 15 张） | 给妇女现金以换取其共同责任 | "They've experimented, for example, with what we called conditional cash transfer"（W7 23:16–23:18） |
| 微型金融（microfinance） | "To "empower" women by giving them micro-loans: access to credit"（L7 第 15 张） | 以小额贷款让妇女取得信贷 | "Some of you would have heard of Bangladesh and the Grameen Bank experiment"（W7 24:13–24:16） |
| duty-bearers | "women end up as "duty-bearers for household poverty alleviation" (Chant 2016)"（L7 第 16 张） | 妇女成为家庭脱贫的责任承担者 | "they end up being the duty bearers for household poverty alleviation"（W7 25:35–25:37） |
| feminization of responsibility and/or obligation | ""Feminization of responsibility and/or obligation": Entrenchment of non-egalitarian model of the family"（L7 第 16 张） | 责任与义务女性化，固化不平等家庭模式 | "It's still the women who's doing the housework. It's just that, now they get cash for it."（W7 26:49–26:55） |
