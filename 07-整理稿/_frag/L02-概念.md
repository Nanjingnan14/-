## L2 Dependency and Underdevelopment（W2，课件 60 张）

### 概念与理论

| 概念／理论 | 课件定义（张号） | 一句话定义 | 老师解释的原句（录音位置） |
|---|---|---|---|
| modernization approach（现代化取径） | 「EACH COUNTRY IS RESPONSIBLE FOR ITS OWN DEVELOPMENT. RICH COUNTRIES DID THE RIGHT THINGS. POOR COUNTRIES SHOULD EMULATE RICH COUNTRIES.」（第 21 张） | 每国自负发展之责，穷国应模仿富国 | 「The modernization school gave a very simple answer. The poor countries are poor because they are doing the wrong things.」（W2 第一段 17:07） |
| Rostow's five stages of growth（含 take-off） | 「Posited that every country in the world can be placed in one of five stages of economic growth」（第 3 张） | 各国可被置于五个经济增长阶段之一 | 「And you will remember the analogy of an airplane, how you, you know, sort of taxi along the runway until you have speed up enough, you've accumulated all of the necessary preconditions for takeoff.」（W2 第一段 01:32） |
| methodological nationalism | 「each country's development is seen as a discrete and largely self-contained project… It is in that sense a static and ahistorical model.」（第 7 张，Krishna 2009: 14） | 各国发展被视为离散自足且静态非历史 | 「Krishna used the term methodological nationalism, right, saying that it's too focused on each country running in its own lane as though it's a fair competition for everyone but actually it's not the same race track, it's not the same game for all of the countries that are involved.」（W2 第一段 03:12） |
| dependency theory（依附理论） | 「THE UNDERDEVELOPMENT OF POOR COUNTRIES IS THE RESULT OF INTERACTION BETWEEN GLOBAL POLITICS AND ECONOMY.」（第 21 张） | 穷国欠发达源于全球政治经济互动 | 「It emerged from the continent in around the 1960s as a challenge and response to the decline of the modernization school.」（W2 第一段 18:48） |
| dependent development（依附式发展） | 「POOR COUNTRIES ARE LOCKED IN A STATE OF DEPENDENT DEVELOPMENT.」（第 21 张） | 穷国被锁在依赖核心的状态 | 「Poor countries are underdeveloped because they are locked in a state of dependency or dependent development.」（W2 第一段 17:07） |
| underdevelopment（欠发达） | 「Underdevelopment is not natural: it is the result of a long history of colonial domination」（第 26 张） | 欠发达非自然，是长期殖民支配结果 | 「And so under development is not natural. It is the result of a long history of colonial domination.」（W2 第一段 23:57） |
| development 与 underdevelopment 为同一过程两面 | 「Development (of the west) and underdevelopment (of the rest) are two sides of the same process, resulting in polarization」（第 26 张） | 西方发展与其余地区欠发达同源 | 「His argument is development of the West and under development of the West are two sides of the same process.」（原话如此；课件第 26 张作 the rest）（W2 第一段 22:19） |
| colonialism（殖民主义三要素） | 「Extraction of natural resources」「Mobilization of colonial labour」「Underdeveloping industries in colonies」（第 30–33 张三节标题） | 资源抽取、劳动力动员、产业去工业化 | 「So I think there are three main aspects.」（W2 第一段 25:36） |
| export monoculture（出口单作） | 「local farmers produced a single crop for export like peanuts or coffee, reducing agriculture to export monoculture」（第 30 张） | 殖民地农业被缩减为单一出口作物 | 「English and French colonies in the Caribbean in Central America, local farmers were forced to produce a single crop for exports.」（W2 第一段 25:36） |
| metropolis–satellite model（大都市—卫星模型） | 「Originated in the colonial era, when colonial powers implanted new cities in Third World countries to facilitate the transfer of economic surplus to the West」（第 36 张） | 城市层级链把剩余从边缘输往西方 | 「So he argues that there is a geographical dimension to under development which he calls the metropolis satellite model.」（W2 第一段 39:22） |
| surplus（剩余） | 「This “surplus” – the amount of work beyond that needed to meet basic needs – creates profits for the owners」（第 39 张） | 超出基本需要的劳动量形成利润 | 「The surplus is the amount of work beyond what is needed to meet basic needs.」（W2 第一段 46:07） |
| neo-Marxism／monopoly capitalism | 「Argue that capitalism was in a period of “monopoly capitalism” where large companies dominated and exploited poorer countries (Baran and Sweezy 1968)」（第 39 张） | 大公司支配剥削穷国的资本主义阶段 | 「There's only one way out. We need to break away completely from the world capitalist system.」（W2 第一段 48:15） |
| self-reliance（自立） | 「Strategy of self-reliance:… Not to rely on Western aid and technology… Trade with other periphery countries on equal, mutually beneficial terms」（第 40 张） | 不靠西方援助技术，与边缘国平等贸易 | 「We need to adopt a strategy of self-reliance. We cannot rely on Western aid nor can we rely on Western technology.」（W2 第一段 48:15） |
| world-system theory（世界体系理论） | 「While dependency theory focuses on nation-states, Wallerstein takes the whole world system as the unit of analysis」（第 47 张） | 以整个世界体系为分析单位 | 「The world system approach, as you can tell from the name, take the whole world, the whole world economy as the unit of analysis.」（W2 第二段 12:22） |
| core（核心） | 「Core: Most economically advanced, focus on high-skill, capital-intensive production (information, finance, service industries).」（第 49 张） | 最先进、高技能资本密集、能影响边缘 | 「The core, like dependency theory, suggests they are the most economically advanced.」（W2 第二段 14:12） |
| periphery（边陲） | 「Periphery: Least economically developed, focus on low-skill, labor-intensive production and extraction of raw materials.」（第 49 张） | 最不发达、低技能劳动密集与原料抽取 | 「The periphery are the least economically developed focus on low-skilled, labor-intensive production and the extraction of raw materials.」（W2 第二段 16:04） |
| semi-periphery（半边陲） | 「Semi-periphery: Midway between core and periphery. More economically developed and less dependent on core countries than periphery.」（第 49 张） | 介于核心与边缘，较边缘发达少依赖 | 「The purple layer. They lie between the core and the periphery. They are more developed than the periphery, wealthier, and they're also less dependent on the core than the periphery.」（W2 第二段 16:04） |
| 半边陲的缓冲功能 | 「The semi-periphery acts as a buffer between core and periphery… Partially deflects political pressure and prevents periphery countries from forming a united front against the core」（第 50 张） | 半边陲转移政治压力、阻止边缘联合 | 「it plays a very important political function because it deflects the political pressure on the core and it prevents the periphery countries from forming a united front against the core.」（W2 第二段 18:02） |
| upward／downward mobility（上下流动） | 「The three-tiered model allows for potential movement from one category to another… Upward and downward mobility」（第 51 张） | 国家可在三层间上下移动 | 「There is upward and downward mobility.」（W2 第二段 22:20） |
| waste disposal frontier（废物处置边疆） | 「The flow of electronic waste from “core” to “periphery” shows how peripheral zones have become waste-disposal frontiers (Frey 2012)」（第 55 张） | 边缘地带成为核心垃圾的处置边疆 | 「It becomes the waste disposal frontier.」（W2 第二段 39:28） |

### 人名与主张

| 人名 | 是谁 | 主张 | 出处 |
|---|---|---|---|
| W. W. Rostow | 现代化理论代表，《The Stages of Economic Growth》作者 | 各国可被置于五个经济增长阶段之一 | L2 第 3–4 张；W2 第一段 01:32 |
| Krishna | 被引作者，课件只标 Krishna（2009） | Rostow 把各国当作同一跑道上分道起跑的跑者；模型静态、非历史（methodological nationalism） | L2 第 6–7 张；W2 第一段 03:12 |
| Andre Gunder Frank | 依附理论代表；课件第 24 张标题为「FRANK'S DEVELOPMENT OF UNDERDEVELOPMENT」 | 西方发展与其余地区欠发达是同一过程两面；欠发达原因在外部殖民主义与外国支配 | L2 第 24–26、35 张；W2 第一段 20:29（老师称书名 Dependent Accumulation and Underdevelopment、出版年 1962，与课件第 24 张的 1967 不一致，to be verified） |
| Immanuel Wallerstein | 世界体系理论提出者 | 以世界体系而非民族国家为分析单位；世界分 core／semi-periphery／periphery，位置可上下流动 | L2 第 47–51 张；W2 第二段 12:22 |
| Karl Marx | 古典马克思主义者（第 39 张「Classical Marxism」） | 社会分为资产阶级与无产阶级；剩余为所有者创造利润 | L2 第 39 张；W2 第一段 46:07 |
| Baran and Sweezy | 新马克思主义被引作者（1968） | 资本主义进入 monopoly capitalism，大公司支配并剥削较穷国家 | L2 第 39 张 |
| So | 被引作者，课件只标 So（1990: 107；1990） | 现代化与依附两取径在结构、原因、国家联系、方案上极性对立 | L2 第 44–46 张 |
| McMichael | 被引作者，课件只标 McMichael（2005: 45-6） | 西方财富被设为典范；发展被叙述为普遍自然、各国可循同一路径 | L2 第 5 张 |
| Willis | 被引作者，课件标 Willis（2005: 79；2011） | 剩余自村庄经各级城市流向核心；1950s–2000s 各派并置成时间线 | L2 第 38、42–43 张 |
| Bujra | 被引作者（1992） | 东印度公司「成功把印度从制造业国家变为出口原料的国家」 | L2 第 33 张 |
| Frey | 被引作者（2012） | 电子垃圾自核心流向边缘，边缘成为废物处置边疆并承担代价 | L2 第 55 张 |
