## 第 12 张｜EXISTING INSTITUTIONS

EXISTING INSTITUTIONS
Bretton Woods institutions: International Monetary Fund, World Bank
▪China has been dissatisfied with its low level of decision making power in these existing structures
▪The US has veto power in both the IMF and World Bank, but not China
▪China has little say in the World Bank compared with US and Japan

▪1st US: 16%
▪2nd Japan: 6.9%
▪3rd China: 5.8%
▪Similar distribution of voting power in the IMF (as of Sep 2026):

▪2nd Japan: 6.14%

## 第 21 张｜BELT AND ROAD INITIATIVE (BRI)

BELT AND ROAD INITIATIVE (BRI)
▪BRI as “soft balancing”:
▪March Westward strategy: “China has created a Eurasian zone of economic influence in its west in order to offset the imminent threat from the USA from the east.” (Chan 2017: 6)
▪Tackle overcapacity
The Chinese economy has expanded so rapidly that it now produces more than it can consume: enormous excess of manufactured products and construction capacity
Silk Road countries can provide new markets and help absorb Chinese products, e.g. iron, steel, aluminum, shipbuilding products, cement, glass, etc.
Chinese companies can relocate some excessive production capacity to neighboring countries in Southeast Asia

## 第 22 张｜▪Boost regional trade

▪Boost regional trade
▪“Many developing Asian countries are faced with the task of upgrading their totally inadequate and inefficient road, air, transport, port and railroad infrastructure, which is a key barrier to achieving local economic take-off and regional integration” (Yu 2017: 359)
▪Helping Asian countries strengthens cross-border transport → helps China build stronger bilateral trade ties with its Asian neighbours
▪Support development of inland China
