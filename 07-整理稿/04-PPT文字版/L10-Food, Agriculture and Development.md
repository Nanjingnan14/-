## 第 4 张｜GLOBAL FOOD WASTE

GLOBAL FOOD WASTE
One-third of all food produced for human consumption in the world (around 1.3 billion tons) is lost or wasted every year (FAO, 2011)
45% of all fruit and vegetables, 35% of fish and seafood, 30% of cereals, 20% of dairy products and 20% of meat
Most of this wastage occurs in the developed world
Per capita food waste by consumers in Europe and North America is around 95-115kg per year
Compared to just 6-11kg in sub-Saharan Africa and South/South-East Asia (World Economic
Forum, 2015)

## 第 15 张｜GREEN REVOLUTION

GREEN REVOLUTION
1950s-1960s: “Revolution” to remake Third World agriculture through a “package” of plant-breeding agricultural technologies
Introduction of US model of chemical agriculture into Third World
High-yielding varieties of hybrid seeds
Chemical fungicides and pesticides
Intensive irrigation and fertilization
Impact:
Increase in agricultural productivity with dramatic yields
Farmers incorporated into a “chemical treadmill”
Rupture ecological cycle of natural regeneration
[图]

## 第 20 张｜NEW DIVISION OF WORLD AGRICULTURAL LABOUR

NEW DIVISION OF WORLD AGRICULTURAL LABOUR
Rise of land-based agricultural investments
The acquisition or long-term lease of national land by foreign governments or companies for food and fuel production
Products usually exported to investing countries
Dubbed “land grabbing” or the “global land rush”
Third World farmers face new conditions of work
Producing for distant consumers, many of whom are ignorant of the conditions of food production
Need to meet high standards of quality and consistency for marketing purposes that are imposed by corporations

OUR FOOD FUTURES
